SUPPORT · A SNAKE ROGUELIKE

FOUND A BUG? HAVE AN IDEA?

The fastest way to reach us is to open an issue on GitHub. Bug reports, feature requests and rule questions are all welcome. Tell us what happened and what you expected.

COMMON QUESTIONS

How does a run work?Choose a route through the map. Each stage is a different kind of game; clear it and pick an upgrade card. Crash and you lose a snake. Lose your last one and the run is over.

What is the difference between Classic and Modern?Both are Endless mode: one board, no end, played for a high score. Modern has the jump and pick-ups. Classic is Snake exactly as you remember it.

How do I jump?Tap the JUMP button and your snake hops over its own body.

Where are my scores kept?On your device, and in your iCloud account if you are signed in. Sign in to Game Center to put your best scores on the world leaderboards.

THE FINE PRINT

Snake Lab has no ads and no in-app purchases. It collects no personal data, only anonymous gameplay statistics; see the privacy policy.
