PRIVACY · A SNAKE ROGUELIKE

The short version: Snake Lab collects no personal data. No ads, no accounts. It sends anonymous gameplay statistics that help us improve the game.

WHAT THE APP COLLECTS

No personal data. There are no ads and no accounts. Your runs, scores, unlocks and settings live on your device. The only thing the app sends us is the anonymous gameplay statistics described below.

ANONYMOUS GAMEPLAY STATISTICS

To learn which stages are too hard, which upgrades get picked and how long runs last, the app sends short records of play: when the app is opened and closed, the mode and rooms you choose, the cards you pick, stages won, how each snake was lost, and scores and times. Each record also carries the app version, the iPhone model, the iOS version, the game's language and your phone's Region setting.

The records share a random number made when the app is installed, so we can tell how many people play. It is not linked to your name, Apple ID, Game Center account, advertising identifier or location, your IP address is not recorded, and deleting the app discards the number. The records are stored with Microsoft Azure Application Insights, deleted after 90 days, and used only to improve Snake Lab. They are never sold, shared, or used for advertising or tracking.

ICLOUD

If you are signed in to iCloud, your scores and unlocks are kept in your own iCloud account so they follow you to your other devices. Apple stores them under Apple's Privacy Policy; we cannot see them.

GAME CENTER

If you sign in to Apple's Game Center, your best scores are submitted to Apple's leaderboard service under Apple's Privacy Policy. We never see your Game Center identity.

CHILDREN

The app is suitable for all ages and is not directed at children. It collects no personal data from anyone.
