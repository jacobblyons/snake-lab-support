PRIVACY · A SNAKE ROGUELIKE

The short version: Snake Lab collects nothing. No ads, no analytics, no accounts.

WHAT THE APP COLLECTS

Nothing. There are no ads, no accounts and no analytics, and the app makes no network requests of its own. Your runs, scores, unlocks and settings live on your device.

ICLOUD

If you are signed in to iCloud, your scores and unlocks are kept in your own iCloud account so they follow you to your other devices. Apple stores them under Apple's Privacy Policy; we cannot see them.

GAME CENTER

If you sign in to Apple's Game Center, your best scores are submitted to Apple's leaderboard service under Apple's Privacy Policy. We never see or store your Game Center identity or scores on any server of ours; we have no servers.

CHILDREN

The app is suitable for all ages and is not directed at children. It collects no data from anyone.

CHANGES
